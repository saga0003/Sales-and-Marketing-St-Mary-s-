# St. Mary’s marketing workspace: evaluation guide

**Open:** [stmarys-marketing-workspace.vercel.app](https://stmarys-marketing-workspace.vercel.app)

This version combines content review, images, manual research, tasks and documents. GA4 reports and Instagram publishing require connected school accounts. **Setup required** means configuration is still needed.

## First access and your team

Open the personal **owner setup link supplied separately**. Choose a password of at least 12 characters and confirm it. After this single-use setup, sign in with your work email and password. Ask your workspace administrator for a replacement if the link has expired or was already used.

In **Settings → Team & access → Add member**, enter their name, email and role:

- **Contributor:** prepares and submits their own drafts.
- **Manager:** reviews content and manages accounts, connections and publishing.

Copy the member’s personal setup link and share it directly with that colleague. Links expire after 24 hours; no email is sent automatically. Managers can generate recovery links, change roles or deactivate members. Deactivation retains their content and ends their access. **Change password** updates your own password and signs out previous sessions.

## Draft, review and publish

In **Content → Create post**, choose the institution, channels, format and optional planned time. Dates use **India Standard Time**. Attach evidence for exam dates, results or medical-seat claims.

**Media library** accepts JPEG, PNG and WebP images up to 3 MiB each. Uploads become JPEGs; transparent areas become white. Review the saved preview. Contributors select their own uploads and can retain an image a manager attached to their draft. External image links provide planning previews only.

**Save draft** shares the draft with the workspace. A **browser copy** is unsent and device-local. Restore it when prompted, then save or submit.

**Send for review** asks a manager to decide. **Request changes** or **Reject** requires feedback; the author can revise and resubmit. Comments and saved history belong to specific revisions. Editing approved content creates a new revision, clears approval and cancels eligible pending jobs.

- **No publishing destination:** approval stays within the content plan. Selecting “Instagram” as a channel alone does not publish anything.
- **Connected Instagram destination:** **Approve & queue** names the exact accounts and delivery time. Confirming queues the approved image and caption automatically. With no future date, the job is due on the next publishing run. Publishing must be enabled before delivery occurs.

Live publishing currently supports one image in **Post** format, captions up to 2,200 characters, and images at least 320 pixels wide with a ratio from 4:5 to 1.91:1. Scheduled times indicate when a job becomes due; worker backlog and provider processing can delay delivery.

## Connect Google or Meta

Managers start in **Settings → Connections**. Save app credentials there, authorize the service, assign a school, choose the exact property/account and **Test access**. **App ready** is different from **Account connected** or **Data imported**.

- **Google:** a Google Cloud OAuth web app, the exact displayed callback URL, enabled Analytics Admin and Data APIs, and a Google account with access to the school’s GA4 property. This connection reads reports.
- **Meta:** a Meta app with Facebook Login, the displayed callback URL, and a professional Instagram account linked to a Facebook Page you manage. Required permissions are `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `instagram_basic` and `instagram_content_publish`; app review or appropriate app-role access is required.

For GA4, open **Performance → Website analytics**, choose the property and date range, then **Import report**. Check the report’s period and import timestamp. CSV exports retain attribution and dates. GA4 key events reflect the property’s configuration; they are not automatically confirmed admissions.

## Delivery results and remaining work

**Queued** means waiting; **Publishing** means processing; **Confirmed published** includes the provider result. A **Failed** job shows its reason and can be retried after correction. Cancelled jobs can be resumed while their revision remains approved. **Confirmation needed** means delivery may have succeeded: inspect Instagram and use the verification flow, rather than resending. Managers can process the next due job from Content’s publishing queue; this action covers all schools.

Intelligence watchlists and research briefs require manual checking. Managers verify evidence before converting useful briefs into drafts. Workspace provides task boards/lists and searchable text documents.

Automatic exam/news collection, competitor monitoring, SEO/GEO/AEO services, ads reporting or spending, additional publishing platforms/formats, AI generation and Notion-style blocks/live editing remain future integrations.
